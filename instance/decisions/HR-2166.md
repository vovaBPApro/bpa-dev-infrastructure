## What it requires

Today HR-1726 caps review at three rounds and the row then parks. The operator's
ruling inserts a phase before the park:

1. Three review rounds, unchanged.
2. If the row is still not accepted, the lane gets **up to three further rounds
   whose purpose is proof, not redesign** — run the tests, produce debug logs,
   reproduce the reviewer's finding on its own SHA. The same practice a product
   coder follows: the person who wrote the change demonstrates it works.
3. Only if it still fails review does the row park and come to the operator —
   which the operator explicitly confirms is correct behaviour and should stay:
   *"ти його не доробляєш, просто йдеш до мене і питаєш."*

## Why it is right, in the orchestrator's words rather than the operator's

Every rejection recorded on this installation so far has been rejected for
**missing or unexecuted evidence**, not for a wrong idea. V3-0.15 round 1: a
documented command that could not run as written. V3-0.20 round 1: six findings
closed and one measurement unreproducible. V3-0.23 rounds 1 and 2: a bound whose
own locks ran at a budget where the defect was arithmetically impossible.
V3-0.29 round 3: four blocking findings, all outside what the new locks assert,
with both suites green. V3-1.9a rounds 1 and 2: the destructive path never
executed against a real account.

In every one of those the design survived and the evidence did not. A phase that
spends rounds on proving rather than rewriting is therefore aimed at the actual
failure mode, and it is cheaper than a recut: the work already exists.

## Boundaries

- A proving round **may not change the decision under review**. If a proving round
  concludes the design is wrong, that is a finding and the row goes back to
  redesign — it does not silently become round four of the same argument.
- A proving round is still subject to Hard Floor 7. Producing a log is not
  producing a pass; an unmeasured subject stays unmeasured.
- The park is not removed. It moves.
- This does not change who may release a park. That is V3-0.29, and its round-3
  review found the current mechanism unsafe.
