## Ruling

**Five lanes, not three.** Everything else in HR-2342 and HR-2398 is unchanged: the cap is
per repository, it remains a **ceiling and not a target**, and reliability still outranks
throughput.

## The measurement, stated because five is the edge and not a safe middle

HR-2342's cap of three rested on a measurement, and that measurement is now load-bearing in
a way it was not before. Row V3-0.34 records this host saturating at **five** concurrent
lanes — 12 cores, load average 16.46 — with the test suite stretching under load
(98s idle → 112.87s at six workers → 115.19s at twelve, where the deadline then killed a
passing run).

So five is precisely the point at which the host was measured to saturate. Running there is
his call and is authorised; the orchestrator's obligation is to notice if the evidence
degrades. **The suite is this system's measurement instrument** — if lane count above
capacity stretches it until timing kills passing runs, the fleet gets faster and its results
get less trustworthy at the same moment. Watch the suite's runtime at five and report a
degradation rather than absorbing it.

The derived budget remains V3-0.34, still open. This ruling sets a number; it does not
derive one.

## Live interaction, recorded because it is the point of HR-2451

The V3-2.15 lane was **already running** when this arrived, implementing the `fleet.floor`
correction against a cap of three. A systemd lane takes no new instruction, so it could not
be told.

It should survive the change anyway, and the reason is the design it was given rather than
luck: its brief forbids substituting one underived constant for another and directs it to
V3-2.11's B3 semantics, where "idle with work outstanding" (**0** running) is decoupled from
"below a target" and the target knob defaults to **off**. If it followed that, the cap is a
number quoted to the orchestrator, not a threshold compiled into behaviour, and moving it
from three to five changes a value rather than a design.

**Verify that when it reports.** If it hardcoded three, it needs a follow-up — and that is
evidence for HR-2451, not against the lane.
