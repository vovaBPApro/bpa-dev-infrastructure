## Disposition

This is the trust cost of false greens, stated directly. It is the operator
telling us the damage is not the individual bug — it is that a green report no
longer means anything to him.

- **Restart:** done. The orchestrator was restarted (daemon + orch, Opus 5, MCP
  connected) and proved live with a voice+document test.
- **The underlying cause is mechanism, not apology.** Per
  `instructions/operator-feedback.md`, a missing mechanism gets a mechanism.
  Landed since: `land-branch.sh` (refuses merges from a linked worktree and
  refuses a merge whose HEAD did not move), fail-closed edge tests, the lint
  baseline driven 39 -> 0 with a regression lock, and red-proof required for
  every lock (break it, show it fail, restore).
- **Not yet closed:** the workboard "29 open rows" was a number that could not
  change — exactly the class of fake signal that produced this message. Fix in
  flight (`workboard-truth`).
