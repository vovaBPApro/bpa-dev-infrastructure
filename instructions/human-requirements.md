# Human Requirements

For every Human-directed concept, plan, backlog item, or defect, copy the
Human's original words verbatim into a clearly labelled source block. Keep
generated interpretation, decisions, and implementation text separate.

- Preserve the verbatim block unchanged throughout drafting and review. No
  agent may reword, clean up, edit, or prune it.
- Only the designated final authority may finalize or prune that block, and
  must do so as an explicit finalization act.
- Keep the source block with the durable artifact and link derived acceptance
  criteria back to it.
- The Human retains product priority and go/no-go authority. Do not treat
  drafting, review, or implementation approval as a substitute for that
  decision.
