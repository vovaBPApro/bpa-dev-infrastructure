## 1. Security exception — authorized, bounded, and on the record

`instance/handoff-oldorch-2026-07-30.md` §5 carries a hard rule, hand-carried
from Vova himself: **never move or share private SSH keys between hosts — public
halves only.**

Here Vova explicitly overrides it: "Даю добро на виключний кейс. Даю дозвіл тобі
перенести ключі один раз." The orchestrator raised the conflict with him plainly
first (that the transfer would contradict a rule he set, and that no transfer was
needed because a locally-generated key already existed and only its public half
had to reach GitHub). He reaffirmed. As product owner that is his decision.

Bounds that stay in force, because he authorized a transfer and nothing wider:
- **One time.** This is an exception, not a new standing model. It must not
  quietly become the way credentials are provisioned.
- The durable fix is still a credential that `bpa-infra` owns. Achieving push
  does not close that.
- The rule in §5 is otherwise unchanged for every other host and key.

Context for why he was short with us: he had no laptop available, and two agents
approached him about the same 30-second task. That coordination failure is ours.

## 2. Restore `/model` in the daemon

The new daemon has no `/model` handler at all — verified: the command chain in
`daemon/server.ts` holds only `/start_codex`, `/start_claude`, `/session`,
`/status`, `/done`, `/mission_done`, `/mission`, `/help`, `/screen`, `/compact`,
`/kill`, `/exit`, `/restart`. His `/model fable` therefore fell through to the
orchestrator as plain text.

This blocks the agreed model posture (thin top on a lean tier, Fable as the
escalation tier — `instance/base-expectations-2026-07-30.md`): with no command
and no self-switch from inside a session, there is currently no way to switch at
all. Lane `ag-model-command` is building it; the old orchestrator has been asked
for the original implementation to port rather than reinvent.

## 3. The migration-gap audit — the important one

`/model` is proof that features were lost silently. He does not want to discover
the rest one stubbed toe at a time.

Audit sources on this side: `migration-prep/REFERENCE_DAEMON_SNAPSHOT.md`,
`migration-prep/DAEMON_MIGRATION_PLAN.md`,
`migration-prep/DAEMON_RUNTIME_INVENTORY.md`, and
`migration-prep/reference-daemon/`. Those allow an intended-vs-landed diff
without touching the old host.

The known blind spot: anything that existed on the old host but was never written
into those parity docs is invisible to that diff — which is exactly how `/model`
was lost. The old orchestrator has been asked for its own inventory of the old
command surface to cover that gap.

`state: open` until the audit reports and the gaps are either restored or
explicitly declined.
