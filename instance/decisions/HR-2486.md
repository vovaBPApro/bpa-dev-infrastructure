# HR-2486 — The eyes reaction is his receipt: it means received AND stored

## Human words (verbatim, do not reword)

> 1. Твоє повідомлення взагалі не дійде (телеграм відвалився). Ми не знаємо того, чого не отримали - ну я собі так розумію - якщо на моєму повідомлені є реакція (очі) то я вважаю що ти його отримав і зберіг
>
> Виправляється легко — одна регулярка в контролері - то виправляй, а не відволікай мене

## Ruling

**The 👀 reaction is a receipt with a defined meaning, chosen by him: *received and
stored*.** Not "delivered to a session", not "seen by the daemon". If the eyes are on his
message, the message is on disk and will survive a restart.

This closes the first residual risk by his own design rather than mine. He cannot audit
what never arrived — no system can — but he can audit what he *sent*, because he still has
his own outbox. The reaction converts an unanswerable question ("did it arrive?") into one
he can settle by looking at his own screen. That is why it is the right mechanism: it puts
the check where the evidence already is.

## Why this is a defect today and not merely a new feature

Measured in `daemon/server.ts` before dispatching the fix:

- **`:3725`** — `appendInboxLine(INSTALL_ROOT, {...})` is wrapped in
  `try { … } catch { /* best-effort; never block delivery on mirroring */ }`. A failed
  write — full disk, bad permissions, a relocated state dir — is swallowed whole.
- **`:3767`** — the `access.ackReaction` site fires with no knowledge of that outcome.
- **`:3885`** — the 👀 site fires after a successful `tmuxPasteText`, which proves
  *delivery to the session*, not storage.

So the reaction he has just declared to mean "stored" can currently appear on a message
that was never stored. This is the repository's named recurring defect — **absence
rendering as success** — sitting on the one channel he uses to run the system.

The comment "never block delivery on mirroring" was a correct trade when the reaction meant
nothing. It is the wrong trade now: the reaction is load-bearing, so the write it claims to
attest must be checked.

## What must change

1. **The 👀 reaction is emitted only after `appendInboxLine` returns successfully.** Both
   reaction sites obey this; no path may ack a message whose mirror write failed or was
   skipped.
2. **Delivery still must not be blocked.** He must not lose a message because the mirror
   broke. Deliver, do not ack, and tell him — the fix is to stop *claiming* storage, not to
   stop working.
3. **A mirror failure is loud.** Swallowing it is what created this. On failure he gets a
   pinged message naming the message id that was not stored, so he knows which of his own
   sent messages to re-send.
4. **The meaning is recorded where a future reader will find it** — the reaction sites and
   `daemon/inbox-mirror.ts` both carry the contract, because the next person to add a
   reaction must know it is a receipt and not decoration.

## What this does not close

A message that never reaches the daemon at all produces no reaction, which is correct and
is the point — but it is *also* what a message in flight looks like for its first second,
and what a stopped daemon looks like forever. The reaction distinguishes stored from
not-stored; it does not distinguish not-yet from never. He reads that gap correctly already
("ми не знаємо того, чого не отримали"), and the honest answer is that only his own outbox
closes it.
