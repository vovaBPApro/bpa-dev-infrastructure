## What was deleted and what survives it

- **993 lane branches** and **336 archive refs** created earlier today, then
  `git gc --prune=now`. Their names, full tip SHAs, dates and subjects are committed
  in `instance/evidence/purged-v2-lanes-2026-08-03.tsv` and
  `instance/evidence/purged-archive-refs-2026-08-03.tsv`.
- **1700 orphaned worktree directories** under `/root/.cache/infra-lanes`. Live
  worktree paths were read from `git worktree list --porcelain` and excluded by exact
  match before anything was removed — no live worktree was touched.

The archive refs were deleted deliberately. They existed to make this morning's reap
reversible while the v2 line was still the working line. It no longer is, and keeping
them would have pinned every object against garbage collection on a host he asked to
clean up. After `gc --prune=now` the manifests are a record of what existed, not a
recovery mechanism — the objects are gone. That is the intended outcome of his
instruction, stated here so nobody later mistakes the manifest for a restore path.

`v2-deprecated` is untouched, complete, and pushed to origin. Everything that was ever
merged into the old `main` is still there.

## Consequence for the two in-flight lanes

Both were built against the old `main` and are therefore on the abandoned line:

- `worktree-agent-a4bb8550a6a3b1b39` — stable mission identity (UNIQUE
  `correlation_id`, idempotent create, migration). Round 2 fixed the Tier A defect and
  proved the new test fails before the fix.
- `worktree-agent-a374b3b71f9682a79` — `gate/land.sh --target-branch`, with a test
  proving the flag retargets the freshness guard rather than skipping it.

Landing them into `v2-deprecated` would be pure waste — the exact motion this repo
spent the day diagnosing. All three touched files (`core/state.ts`,
`core/mission-cli.ts`, `gate/land.sh`) exist on v3, so both fixes are portable. They
will be ported to the new `main` rather than landed on the dead line.

Note the ordering trap this creates: `gate/land.sh` on v3 is the same pre-fix copy, so
landing onto any branch other than the repository default is still impossible there.
The `--target-branch` port is therefore the first thing that has to reach v3, and
until it does, v3 landings must go to `main` directly.
