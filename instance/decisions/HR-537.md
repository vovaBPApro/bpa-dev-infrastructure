## Two separate rulings

### 1. Chat capability scope

The site chat is not a support widget. Named capabilities:

- **керувати юай** — drive the UI
- **збирати інфо з бази** — query the database
- **працювати з базою знань** — work with a knowledge base
- **навчати білла через менеджмент правил для метчінгу і категоризації** — train
  Bill by managing matching and categorization rules

He notes the plans describe this thoroughly and they may be used as the starting
point: "Там все в планах дуже ретельно описано. Можна це брати."

He also flags the testing surface explicitly — "Там тестувати є дууууже багато
чого!" — so test scope is part of the requirement, not an afterthought.

The migration inventory records chat as PARTIAL: composer and a text turn exist;
streaming frames, attachments, sessions/history, tool orchestration, sidecar
persona and the live backend are missing. None of the four capabilities above is
currently ported.

### 2. Delivery gate — BINDING ON ALL ROLES

Nothing visual goes to Vova unless a consilium has first analyzed HIS ORIGINAL
WORDS and judged the artifact consistent with them.

    design -> consilium reviews it against his verbatim words -> only then Vova

A consilium that has not read his original wording does not satisfy this. The
point is not a second opinion on aesthetics; it is that his stated requirements
are checked against the artifact by someone other than its author before his time
is spent.

This supersedes the previous practice of sending him screenshots directly, and it
applies to the transaction-form design ([[HR-535]]) and every design after it.
The onboarding design was approved BEFORE this ruling and is not retroactively
invalidated.

Sequence for design work is now: gather -> design -> consilium -> Vova -> implement.
