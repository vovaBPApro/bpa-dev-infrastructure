1. **Nothing missed**: every inbound message must end in exactly one tracked
   disposition — an HR-<msg-id> capture, a workboard row, or an explicit
   answered-in-chat record. The source of truth is the durable
   `instance/decisions/inbox.jsonl` (survives session death), not session
   memory. After any orchestrator restart, reconcile the inbox window since
   the last reconciliation against dispositions before dispatching new work.
2. **Done as he wants**: work items derived from his messages carry his
   verbatim words into the mission artifact (Hard Rule 16); consilium/review
   judges the artifact against the ORIGINAL WORDS (PR-10 pattern), not a
   paraphrase.
3. **10 lanes maintained**: the fleet floor is 10 concurrent lanes while
   tracked open rows exist to feed them; `instance/params.yaml` `fleet.floor`
   must say 10 and the autonomy keepalive enforces it. Falling below the floor
   with open rows available is a defect, not a scheduling choice.
