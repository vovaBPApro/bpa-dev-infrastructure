# HR-2149 — one time, to break the circle

Verbatim (Vova, Telegram 2149, 2026-08-04), replying to the orchestrator's explanation
that waiting for the administrator bot means waiting on something that itself depends on
unparking V3-1.9:

> Та тут на один раз зараз, щоб коло розімкнути, я тобі просто добро даю.
> Я довіряю, що ти зараз фігні не наробиш.
> Ми ж кажемо про структурне рішення у версії три.

Context, his earlier words in the same thread (Telegram 2103), which is why the SSH route
was abandoned:

> Просто з SSH-ключом є велика проблема в тому плані, що я не завжди за комп'ютером.
> Ми тому через Telegram і ведемо розробку, щоб я міг будь-де, будь-коли прийняти
> рішення, дати коментар і направити тебе.

And the decision already on record, which this authorises the execution of:

`decision:v3_1_9_noprogress_park_2026_08_04=unpark_land`

## What this authorises, precisely

Clearing the `no-progress` park on **V3-1.9 only**, once, without an SSH signature.

## What it does not authorise, and what the orchestrator refused

**Hand-deleting the durable attempt refs or hand-editing the round-counter state.** That
was refused when the park first blocked his instruction and is refused again here. It is
indistinguishable from an agent bypassing its own counter, and deleting a ref together
with its mirror is precisely the attack the mirror design exists to detect. A trust
granted is not a reason to remove the evidence that trust was needed.

His sentence "я довіряю, що ти зараз фігні не наробиш" is the reason to build the path
carefully, not the reason to skip building it.

## How it will be executed

The authority becomes **a decision file tracked on `origin/main`** — this file — rather
than a signature. That is not weaker than it sounds, because it reuses the trust root the
repository already enforces:

- `instance/decisions/` content reaches `origin/main` only through the landing gate, which
  requires an independent review;
- V3-0.29 round 2 already made the operator-authority path a **reserved path** in the
  payload guard, so a lane that tries to land its own authorisation is refused with
  `LAND step=payload-guard status=fail detail=reserved-path`.

So a lane cannot mint this file for itself, and the orchestrator cannot either without
passing review. The mechanism is built by a lane, attacked by an independent reviewer, and
only then applied by the orchestrator citing the decision id above — never by the change
that creates the power.

## What remains the structural answer

**HR-2109** — the administrator bot running as root, which becomes the authority once
non-root lanes exist. This row is the bootstrap that makes that possible, and it should be
readable later as exactly that: a one-time, loudly recorded exception, not a precedent.
