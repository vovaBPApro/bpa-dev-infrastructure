## The decision

He approved the orchestrator's counter-proposal ("мені подобаєтьс твоє бачення,
тож рухайся так як ти запропонував"). The ruling as approved:

1. **Rest on `claude-sonnet-5`** — not Fable, not Opus.
2. **Lanes route to Codex**, which is where the headroom is. This is already
   binding policy (`vendor-routing.md`: quota and provider health are routing
   signals; run on the provider with headroom) — it simply was not being applied.
3. **GPT top-orchestrator stays an ARMED FALLBACK**, not the resting state.
   Trigger: Claude weekly "All models" crossing ~90% before the Monday reset.
   The path already exists and is designed for exactly this
   (`orchestrator/runtime.env`: `ORCH_CODEX_MODEL=gpt-5.6-sol`,
   `ORCH_CODEX_REASONING_EFFORT=high`, commented "Fallback top orchestrator
   (Anthropic quota exhausted)").
4. **Fable is escalation-only** — switch up for a hairy incident, back down after.

## Why this supersedes his 2026-07-30 "top on Fable" position

His earlier recorded position was «верхній оркестратор має бути на Fable … щоб
квота мінімально використовувалася» (W-11). That is **superseded by his own
evidence**, not overridden: the usage screenshots he sent with 266 show Fable is
his TIGHTEST bucket, so resting there would consume precisely the reserve he
asks to keep for hard problems. Same goal, corrected instrument.

## The leak this exposed

The record claimed the box ran the thin posture on `claude-fable-5`; the host
actually pinned `claude-opus-5`, the heaviest tier. `bash orchestrator/launch.sh
model` → `claude_model=claude-opus-5` (ground truth, 2026-07-31). So the "thin
top" was never in force. Corrected in `instance/params.yaml` in the same commit
as this row.
