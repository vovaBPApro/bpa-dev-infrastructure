## Why it did not land — the round 3 blocking finding

`bootstrap/install.sh:362-365, 376-378`. `unit_publication_signal` resets HUP/INT/TERM
to their default dispositions *before* rollback begins. A second TERM delivered while
the rollback's first `cp` is in progress terminates the installer with 143, emits
neither `verdict=rolled-back` nor `verdict=rollback-failed`, and leaves the already
published unit at its new bytes.

Reproduced by the reviewer, not argued:

```text
$ /tmp/s3-2-double-signal-probe.sh /tmp/s3-2-review.tZrknD
Terminated
DOUBLE_SIGNAL_RC=143
--- output ---
STATE_IDENTICAL=no
@@ destination snapshot @@
-first.service 126cffecc839475dda9a551eb7c0c01e71cdaff775bac4714fe6510b44e48021
+first.service 767f2ba2b7890aa77756c413ae74d38f99dcad2ad8891ce488c6daf1f92be8e3
```

So the rollback is itself interruptible into a half-restored state, and an unprovable
rollback is indistinguishable in output from a successful one. On a real host that is a
control plane where some units point at the new layout and some at the old, with nothing
reporting it, because each file is individually valid. That is the 2026-08-01 shape
exactly, and it is why this could not be waved through on a "close enough" judgement.

## The lock the recut must satisfy

The recut is not "try again". It lands only with an executable regression lock proving
both of these, red against `09082ec` and green at the recut tip:

1. A second signal delivered **during** rollback still ends with the destination set
   byte-identical to its prior state, including units that did not exist before, which
   must be restored as absence rather than as empty files.
2. When restoration cannot be completed, the installer says so distinguishably —
   a `rollback-failed`-style verdict, never silence and never an exit that looks like
   an ordinary abort. `gate/land.sh` already carries this distinction from V3-0.3
   (`land_fail_rollback`, `verdict=rollback-failed`); follow it rather than inventing a
   second convention.

The likely shape is to keep rollback non-interruptible for its duration — block or
defer the signals rather than resetting them to default — and to make the terminal
verdict a single choke point that every exit path passes through.
