## What this changes

Statutory reporting was previously scheduled as a LATER layer (HR-330: source
documents first, statutory afterwards). This ruling does not reschedule statutory
reporting as a whole — it says the statutory REQUIREMENTS live inside the
transaction create/edit form, so the form cannot be designed as managerial-only
and extended later. Bill must enter, sync and match statutory data together with
all managerial transactions.

He calls it "самоі важливої форми" — the single most important form.

## Explicit latitude

He states porting may NOT be the right answer here — "можливо тут не варто
переносити функціонал, хоча якісь шматки може і варто". This is the one area where
rebuilding is sanctioned, unlike the shell and Bill surfaces where he has twice
corrected us for not porting. The design must say per capability whether it is
ported, rebuilt, or dropped, and why.
