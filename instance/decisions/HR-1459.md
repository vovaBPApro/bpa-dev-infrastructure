# HR-1459 — Role-first consortium model routing (amends HR-212)

Verbatim (Vova, 2026-08-02):

> під час консиліума окремі роді можна піднімати на антропік, підбирати модель
> яка максімально пасуватиме ролі та задачі незалежно від провайдера... можна
> це зафіксувати в інструкіях

## Interpretation of record

`instance/decisions/HR-212.md` pinned the default review consilium to GPT
models specifically ("консиліуму з різних ролей на гпт моделі"). This directive
lifts that provider pin: an individual consilium seat may be raised on
Anthropic (or any other available provider) when that provider/model is the
best fit for the seat's role and task, independent of which provider hosts the
rest of the consilium. Role/persona diversity (HR-212) remains the primary
axis; provider choice per seat is now a capability-fit decision, not a fixed
default.

This does not relax the quota-asymmetric routing default (HR-210, "Provider-
economy default" in `instructions/vendor-routing.md`) or session-independence
(`review-policy.md`): a scarce-provider seat still needs a recorded
justification, and every seat is still a separate session.

Codified in `instructions/vendor-routing.md` (new "Role-first model selection"
section).
