## The one open blocker

`gate/land.sh:324-338`; `instructions/review-policy.md:96-100`.

The gate atomically pushes both durable attempt refs at `:324`, verifies refs and target
SHA at `:328-333`, and runs `review-rounds.ts check --defer-park-exit` at `:335-337` —
but `review_round_park_pending` is not set until **`:338`**. So an exit inside that
window (a persisted ref/mirror/target mismatch at `:333`, or a failure of the check
itself) **counts the attempt and emits no blocker step or reason**, while the policy
text promises one unconditionally.
