# HR-735 — Questions to him: clarifying only, and only if he hasn't already answered

date: 2026-08-01
source: Telegram, message 735 (voice)
status: binding

## Human words (verbatim, do not reword)

> Питання мені можна ставити, але тільки уточнюючи і тільки тоді, коли ти
> переконався, що я на нього ще ні разу не відповідав.

Context: given during the same session as HR-709/HR-681, after repeated
frustration that requirements he had already stated (verbatim, in
transaction-form-vova-verbatim.md; in HR-681 on donor `dev` branches) were
either not checked against before acting, or re-asked instead of looked up.

## Ruling

1. Questions to him are allowed only as **clarifying** questions — not
   open-ended requirement-gathering he has already done.
2. Before asking, **check whether he already answered it** — in this
   session's history, `instance/decisions/inbox.jsonl`, the orch-mailbox
   archive (`instance/README.md` §"Where the full Telegram history actually
   lives"), captured HR-<msg-id>.md rows, and product-side verbatim capture
   docs (e.g. `transaction-form-vova-verbatim.md`) — before sending the
   question.
3. This sharpens, not replaces, Hard Rule 14 ("ask the Human almost never" —
   irreversible/product decisions only). This row adds a precondition even
   for the questions Hard Rule 14 already permits: confirm the answer isn't
   already on record.
