# HR-2342 — Maximum three lanes in parallel

date: 2026-08-04
source: Telegram, message 2342
status: binding
lane_cap: 3

## Human words (verbatim, do not reword)

> І давай трошки повільніше рухатись, менше лайнів в паралель запускай.Давай там до трьох, бо щось когось горить швидко.Давай краще повільніше рухатись, менше проблем буде.

Context — sent while the orchestrator was standing up a five-member consilium to
plan a 10–12 hour sprint toward cutover, and after a day in which the
orchestrator itself died and had to be recovered by hand.

## Ruling

1. **At most three lanes run in parallel.** This replaces the "fleet floor of
   10" that the autonomy nudge asserts and that no one ever derived. Three is a
   ceiling, not a target: fewer is allowed whenever the work does not need them.
2. The stated reason is the operator's own and is part of the ruling, not
   commentary: moving slower produces fewer problems. Do not trade the cap back
   for throughput because a particular sprint looks tight.

## Why this is not merely a throttle

It is corroborated by the repository's own measurement. Row V3-0.34 records the
host saturating at **five** lanes — 12 cores, load average 16.46 — and the test
suite stretching under load (98s idle → 112.87s at six workers → 115.19s at
twelve, where the deadline then killed a passing run). The suite is this
system's measurement instrument, so lane count above the host's capacity does
not merely slow the fleet, it corrupts the evidence the fleet produces. A cap is
therefore a correctness control as much as a comfort one.

## Consequence for planning

A 10–12 hour sprint is worth roughly **30–36 lane-hours**, not the 100+ that a
ten-lane assumption implies. What earns a slot becomes the decisive question of
any sprint plan rather than a secondary one. Plans whose items all queue behind
the single serial landing lock are proportionally more expensive under this cap,
which raises the value of fixing that lock rather than working around it.

The five-member consilium in flight when this ruling arrived was briefed with the
superseded "~10 lanes" assumption. Two members had already reported; the two
still running were sent the correction mid-flight and the synthesis re-scopes the
rest. Recorded here so a later reader does not mistake those two reports'
lane-hour arithmetic for current guidance.

Supersedes the hardcoded fleet floor for operational purposes; **V3-0.34 remains
open**, because deriving the limit from measured host capacity is still the
right mechanism — this ruling sets the number in the meantime rather than
answering the row.
