# HR-1726 — Cap review rounds, escalate to tests

Verbatim (Vova, Telegram 1726, 2026-08-03):

> Є таке відчуття що ми на ревью дуже сильно застрягаємо. Реально треба ставити кап
> в перекидати фічу на тестування. Дуже часто тести давали кращі результати ніж
> ревью. Це ж треба закласти і у в3

## Interpretation of record

Two rulings, both binding:

1. **A review round cap exists.** An item may not be re-reviewed without bound. Past
   the cap, another review round is not an available action.
2. **The escalation target is executable testing, not more review.** His stated
   reason is empirical: tests have produced better results than review rounds on this
   project. So at the cap the open concerns become executable locks, and the item is
   decided by whether those locks pass — not by another reviewer's opinion.

And a scope instruction: this is not a stop-gap for the current host. It must be
built into v3 as well, so the rewrite does not reintroduce the unbounded loop.

## Evidence this directive is responding to

Measured the same day, at `1fef8c8` and after:

- 893 of 1314 unmerged branches are `[REVIEW]` tips — more than two review branches
  per coder branch.
- The `authority` epic alone spent rounds r6 through r23 across 184 branches and
  never landed. At r22 the security lens returned ACCEPT and was re-spawned anyway at
  r23, because a fix elsewhere produced a new SHA and no rule carried its ACCEPT
  forward.
- Estimated 25–35% of overnight quota went into rounds that produced no landed change.

See `instance/consilium-2026-08-03-sprint-review.md` §2a.

## Boundary this ruling does not cross

The cap ends *review rounds*; it does not lower evidence. Hard Floor 7 still holds —
green is fail-closed, and an unmeasured subject is never a pass. Escalating to tests
means the open concern must become an executable lock that fails before the fix and
passes at the reviewed SHA. It does not mean landing an item because review ran out
of turns. A concern that cannot be expressed as a runnable lock is a design problem
and parks the item for recut, which is the other permitted exit at the cap.

Codified in `instructions/review-policy.md` ("Round cap and test escalation").
