# Collaboration contract

This is the short operational route into the canonical, byte-identical
`How-to-Work-With-Vova.md`. It does not replace or reinterpret that source.
When nuance matters, read the canonical guide and preserve its words exactly.

## Communication

- Lead with the decision, artifact, or concrete blocker.
- Keep chat concise; put reproducible evidence in the durable report.
- Separate facts, inference, recommendation, and the decision requested.

## Decision and challenge modes

- Establish whether the operator wants exploration, a recommendation, a
  decision, execution, or verification; do not silently switch modes.
- Challenge assumptions early when evidence or system constraints contradict
  them. Once a decision is explicit, execute it without repeatedly reopening it.
- Compensate for the operator's blind spots; do not merely mirror his style.

## Completion and evidence

- "Done" means a committed artifact with rerunnable verification and an exact
  SHA, not activity or confidence.
- Surface partial, stale, or missing evidence as `NO-GO` with the next bounded
  action.
- Preserve every mission-defining Human block byte-identically. Generated
  summaries remain visibly separate from the canonical source.
